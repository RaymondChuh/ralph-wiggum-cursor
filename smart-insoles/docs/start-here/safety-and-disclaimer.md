# Safety and disclaimer

## Not medical advice

This project and its tutorials are for **education and training insight only**. The insole
**estimates** running metrics. It does **not** diagnose, treat, monitor or prevent any disease,
injury or condition, and it is not a medical device. Research on gait metrics and injury is mixed
and mostly shows associations, not causes; we describe it that way. If you have pain or an injury,
see a qualified health professional.

## Building safely

- **Low voltage only.** Everything runs from USB 5 V or a small lithium battery. Never connect
  anything to mains power.
- **Lithium batteries can catch fire if damaged or charged badly.** Never put a battery under your
  foot. Use protected cells and proper chargers. Never charge while wearing the insole, and never
  leave a battery charging unattended. Stop using any battery that is swollen, hot or damaged.
- **Don't run while plugged in.** Cables to a laptop or charger are trip hazards, and a mains
  connection is a bad idea when you're sweaty. Use battery power for anything you wear while
  moving.
- **Soldering irons are hot (~300–400 °C).** Use a stand, work in a ventilated space, and wash your
  hands afterwards.
- **Skin and sweat.** Insulate all conductors so nothing electrical touches your skin. Stop if
  anything feels warm.
- **Treadmills.** Use the safety key, start slowly, and keep cables clear.

Each hands-on chapter repeats the specific hazards right before the risky step.

## Your data

Gait recordings are personal health-related data. The project keeps raw recordings off the
internet. Only small, de-identified samples are published.
